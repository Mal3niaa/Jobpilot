FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is JobPilot free to use? +

Yes — JobPilot is free to try. You can upload your CV, add jobs, and get AI analysis without a credit card.

How does the AI match score work? +

We compare your structured CV (skills, experience, education, languages) against the job description. The final score is a weighted combination of five components: technical skills (40%), experience (20%), education (10%), languages (10%), and job-specific requirements (20%).

Does the AI decide whether I should apply? +

No. JobPilot shows you evidence — strong matches, partial matches, missing skills, and recommendations. The final decision is always yours.

Which file formats are supported for my CV? +

PDF is supported in the current version. DOCX and other formats are on the roadmap.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Your CV and jobs are visible only to you — protected by account authentication. We never share your data with third parties.
